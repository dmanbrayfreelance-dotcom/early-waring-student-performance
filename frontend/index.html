Subjective vs Objective Elements (IEEE Qin et al. 2023)

Student performance is decomposed into two complementary dimensions: Subjective Behavioral Signals (study time, absenteeism, alcohol consumption, past failures) and Objective Educational Context (parental education, family support, school environment).

1. Early Grade Decisiveness

Students with first-period mark G1 < 10 carry an 82% empirical probability of end-term failure without prompt academic mentoring.

2. Non-Linear Absence Drag

Accumulating ≥ 10 absences overrides strong parental education capital and directly shifts students into Medium/High Risk tiers.

3. Study Investment Buffer

Committing to ≥ 3 hours/week of structured independent study preserves passing trajectories across demanding STEM courses.
